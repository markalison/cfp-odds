How these odds work

  • Every remaining regular-season game is played out 20,000 times. Each game is decided by the rating gap plus 2.2 points for the home team, with 12 points of game randomness, and each team’s strength also drifts by 2.6 points per simulated season.
  • Ratings are grouped by how they calculate. Point-based: ESPN’s BPI, Sagarin, TeamRankings and this site’s points rating (this season’s scores, margins capped at 25, adjusted for opponent and home court). Recent form: TeamRankings’ last five games and Sagarin’s recent rating. The default averages one rating from each source: ESPN BPI, Sagarin Rating, TeamRankings Predictive, Points rating (this site).
  • Seeding goes by record, then head-to-head among the tied teams, then a coin toss. The NBA’s further tiebreakers, like division and conference record, aren’t modeled.
  • Seeds 1-6 in each conference go straight to the playoffs. Seeds 7-10 play in: 7 hosts 8 for the 7 seed, 9 hosts 10, and the 7/8 loser hosts the 9/10 winner for the 8 seed. Every round after that is best of seven, with the better seed at home in games 1, 2, 5 and 7, and no reseeding.
  • Playoffs after a win or loss show the chance to make the playoffs if the team wins or loses its next game.
