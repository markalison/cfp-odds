How these odds work

  • Every remaining regular-season game is played out 20,000 times. Each game is decided by the rating gap plus 0.2 goals for the home team, with 2.3 goals of game randomness, and each team’s strength also drifts by 0.25 goals per simulated season. 22% of games go to overtime or a shootout, and the loser still gets a point.
  • Ratings are in goals a game better than an average team. Sagarin’s ratings, MoneyPuck’s expected goals (expected goals for minus against per game, all situations) and this site’s goals rating (this season’s scores, margins capped at 4, overtime games as one-goal games, adjusted for opponent and home ice). Recent form: Sagarin’s recent rating. The default averages Sagarin Rating, MoneyPuck expected goals, Goals rating (this site).
  • Standings go by points, then regulation wins, then wins, then head-to-head points, then a coin toss.
  • The top three in each division make the playoffs, plus two wild cards per conference. The division winner with more points plays the second wild card and the other plays the first; second plays third in each division. Every round is best of seven, with more points holding home ice in games 1, 2, 5 and 7.
  • Playoffs after a win or loss show the chance to make the playoffs if the team wins or loses its next game.
