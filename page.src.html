Playoff rules applied

  • Automatic bids go to the ACC, Big Ten, Big 12 and SEC champions.
  • One more goes to the highest-ranked champion from the American, Conference USA, MAC, Mountain West, Pac-12 or Sun Belt.
  • Notre Dame gets an automatic bid if ranked in the top 12, leaving six at-large bids. Otherwise there are seven.
  • Seeds follow the committee ranking. Seeds 1-4 get byes, and 5-8 host first-round games.
  • Automatic qualifiers ranked outside the top 12 go at the bottom of the seeding.
  • The bracket is fixed with no reseeding: 1 plays 8/9, 4 plays 5/12, 2 plays 7/10, 3 plays 6/11.

How a season is simulated

    Limits to keep in mind

    • The committee is a model. Its weights were tuned against one ranking, the current one, and have not been tested on past seasons.
    • Conference tiebreakers are simplified to head-to-head, then overall losses, then rating. Real tiebreakers go further.
    • Games in progress use the current score and time left, without possession or field position. The page checks ESPN when it loads, so reload for newer scores.
    • Team logos come from ESPN and belong to their schools.
    • FCS opponents without a rating use a low default, so those games are close to certain wins.
    • Win-out odds marked † come from a separate forced run because too few normal simulations had that team winning. Teams with 5+ losses (6+ outside the power conferences) don't get that run and show a floor value.
